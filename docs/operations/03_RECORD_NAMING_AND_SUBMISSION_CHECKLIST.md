# EA-CSC Record Naming & Six-Point Submission Checklist

## 1. File Naming Convention

Before uploading to the Officer Workspace:

- Use descriptive, ASCII-safe filenames without slashes or special control characters:
  `<EACSC-ID>_v<Version>_<Short-Descriptive-Title>.<ext>`
  Example: `EACSC-2026-ACT-0014_v1_Engineering-Week-Guidelines.pdf`
- Accepted formats: **PDF** (`.pdf`), **Word** (`.docx`), **PNG** (`.png`), **JPEG** (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`).
- Maximum file size: **10 MB** (`10,000,000` bytes). Magic-byte verification rejects spoofed extensions.

## 2. Pre-Submission Author Checklist

1. Confirm the target record's category (`GOV`, `SEC`, `ACT`, `FIN`, `FRM`, `TRN`) and audience (`Public` vs. `Restricted`).
2. Verify the uploaded document matches the version summary and `effective_from` / `effective_until` dates.
3. Click **Submit for Review** once Draft notes and attachment are verified.

## 3. Mandatory Six-Point Reviewer Verification Checklist

Every `For Review` version must have all six items explicitly marked `Confirmed` or `Not Applicable` (with no `Correction Required` items) before substantive approval is permitted:

|  #  | Checklist Key           | Verification Standard                                                             |
| :-: | :---------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1  | `identity_version`      | Record ID, title, version number, and supersession target are accurate.           |
|  2  | `dates_deadlines`       | Effective dates, submission deadlines, and Manila (`UTC+8`) times are consistent. |
|  3  | `location_link`         | Venues, room numbers, or official URLs are verified and active.                   |
|  4  | `audience_requirements` | Target student/officer audience, prerequisites, and eligibility rules are clear.  |
|  5  | `authority_disclosure`  | Issuing council body, signatory authority, and privacy notice are stated.         |
|  6  | `contact_reference`     | Official EA-CSC contact channel and supporting reference IDs are included.        |
