## 2. Public Portal vs. Internal Governance Isolation Guarantees

- **Anonymous callers (`anon`)** are denied direct table `SELECT` on `master_records`, `record_versions`, `record_releases`, `update_bulletins`, `audit_events`, and `turnover_packages` via RLS and revoked privileges.
- **Public access** is exposed strictly through `eacsc_public_catalog`, `eacsc_public_record_detail`, `eacsc_public_withdrawal_notice`, `eacsc_public_bulletins`, `ea-csc-public-download`, and `ea-csc-bulletin-file` (which requires an active officer JWT when `audience = 'internal'`).
- **Internal and restricted documents** (`access_class IN ('internal', 'restricted')`) cannot be published as public releases or included in `public` Update Bulletins.
